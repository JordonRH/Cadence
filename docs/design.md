# Cadence design

Borrow functional ideas from Duolingo: short sessions, an obvious next step, approachable language and fast feedback. Use an original editorial layout, an adult-friendly quiet palette and a friendly hedgehog. No copied illustrations, owl branding, competitive boards, streak penalties or persistent notifications.

Five themes: Meadow (sage/cream), Paper (warm neutral), Dusk (dark), Ocean (blue) and High Contrast. Theme switching is available before login; authenticated choices are stored server-side and restored on another device.

The hedgehog belongs in welcome moments and supportive feedback, never in guilt-based reminders. Mascot animation and additional poses can follow later; respect reduced-motion preferences.

Today gives one next action and a time budget. Your Path stores language and purpose. Library and Progress honestly describe their future function until the corresponding learning features exist. Progress will be evidence-based rather than a decorative score.
